# Contributor and AI guidance

- Use `.github/pull_request_template.md` for every PR. Keep explanations blunt
  and short; include concrete validation and material risks.
- Before reviewing a PR, read and follow [.github/AI_REVIEW.md](.github/AI_REVIEW.md)
  for review scope, comment formats, and exact label transitions.
- Mention only the Jira ticket implemented by the PR in its title and body.
  Describe dependencies and follow-ups without other Jira keys or links.
- Do not review your own PR. Leave formal approval and merging to a human.
